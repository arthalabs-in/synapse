================================================================================
 DELIVERY NOTES
================================================================================

- Opening line is a cold hook. "Every large language model will happily
  invent a citation" should land flat and matter-of-fact — no upward
  inflection. The shock comes from the content, not the delivery.

- "Every one of them. Even the best ones." — two short declaratives.
  Punctuation is your friend. The two periods force a small pause that lets
  it land.

- "That's not a tool. That's a liability." — deliberately harder consonants.
  If the first take is too soft, lower Stability to 35 and regenerate this
  sentence alone.

- Architecture section (Act 2) is the only place with real density. Keep
  Stability higher here (55+) so the model doesn't drift on the multi-clause
  sentences.

- "Report version two. Same claims. Same sources. But now..." — the three
  short sentences are the emotional peak. Do not let ElevenLabs smooth them
  into one breath. If it does, manually split them into separate generations
  and concatenate.

- "Thank you." — last two words should be slightly slower and lower.
  Regenerate just the closing sentence if it comes out rushed.
